# Ontario Change of Use Permit Checklist Generator - Ontario

**City:** Toronto | **Area:** 1600 sq ft | **Expected occupants:** 45

> Preliminary checklist generated with the free open-source Ontario Change of Use Permit Checklist Generator by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga, Ontario. Verify every item with your designer and the local building department before submitting.

## 1. Change of use basics (always required)
- [ ] A change of use permit can be required even with NO construction, when the new use changes the building code classification of the unit
- [ ] Building permit application form plus Schedule 1: Designer Information
- [ ] Zoning verification letter / zoning review: the new use, parking and any patio or outdoor elements must be permitted at the address
- [ ] Existing drawings or a measured survey of the unit as it stands today - the review is against the existing building, not a blank shell
- [ ] A code analysis comparing the old use and the new use: construction type, washrooms, exits, barrier-free, sprinklers and fire alarm

## 2. What the new use usually triggers
- [ ] Washroom count re-checked for the new occupant load - adding a washroom is the most common hidden cost in a change of use
- [ ] Barrier-free entrance and washroom requirements re-checked for the new use
- [ ] Exits and travel distances re-checked; a more hazardous or busier use can require a second exit
- [ ] Fire separations between your unit and neighbours re-checked for the new use rating
- [ ] Parking count re-checked under the zoning by-law for the new use

## Construction also planned
- [ ] Full drawing set for the construction scope: architectural, HVAC, plumbing, electrical and structural where triggered
- [ ] Demolition plan and existing-versus-proposed plans so the examiner can follow the change

## New food use
- [ ] Kitchen exhaust, grease interceptor, gas piping and hood fire suppression reviews if cooking is added - see the restaurant and bakery checklist generators by Dust 2 Engineering Inc.
- [ ] Public health food premises notification before opening

## 3. Mechanical, plumbing and electrical review
- [ ] HVAC capacity and fresh air re-checked for the new occupant load
- [ ] Plumbing fixture count and water service re-checked; backflow preventer where the new use requires one
- [ ] Electrical panel capacity and emergency lighting / exit signs re-checked for the new layout
- [ ] Sprinkler and fire alarm adequacy for the NEW use must be confirmed in writing - never assume the existing system designed for the old use is sufficient

## 4. Other approvals (not part of the building permit)
- [ ] Sign permit (separate) and city business licence for the new use
- [ ] Sector approvals for specific uses: public health for food and personal services, AGCO for alcohol, ministry or college rules for clinics and schools

## Before you submit
- [ ] One combined PDF set: architectural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and structural (if any), all consistent with each other
- [ ] All drawing sets use the same address, unit number, floor area and counts
- [ ] Designs that require an engineer must be stamped by a licensed Ontario P.Eng.; BCIN designer information where applicable
- [ ] Book a pre-consultation or zoning check with the city if the use or layout is not clearly permitted
